## 1. LEAD — IRAN HITS US BASES (Bahrain / Strait of Hormuz)
- Sat night 21 Nov, IRGC operation "Ya Umm al-Banin": missile + drone
  attack on US bases in Bahrain. Tehran CLAIMS it hit two radars of
  US Patriot air-defence systems (al-Riffa / Awali; a second IRGC
  statement named Sheikh Isa / Juffair — inconsistent, presented on
  the page as a claim, not fact).
- Bahrain says the strike hit a CIVILIAN industrial site on Sitra
  Island: fire, 2 wounded, one a Pakistani national in critical
  condition. Bahrain calls it a violation of sovereignty.
- US CENTCOM dismisses the radar-hit claims; says it intercepted two
  one-way attack drones launched from Iran towards Bahrain.
- Trump warns Iran of a "very traumatic" outcome if a nuclear deal
  fails. Al Jazeera (via reports): at least 25 commercial vessels hit
  in or near the Strait of Hormuz over the past 7 days.
- Sources: AFP, Al Jazeera, Middle East Eye, SCMP.

## 2. UKRAINE — 15 DEAD IN KYIV REGION; FIFTH WINTER BEGINS
- Weekend 21–22 Nov strikes killed at least 15 in the Kyiv region:
  Dymer 5-storey block (11 dead), supermarket in Vyshneve (3 dead,
  15 wounded), private home in Bila Tserkva (1 dead).
- Grid under fire as winter sets in; emergency outages across 11
  regions. Zelenskyy presses the US to speed up Patriot interceptor

- Sources: UN News, Euronews, Al Jazeera, AFP.

## 3. CLIMATE FALLOUT — COP31 COLLAPSE REVERBERATES
- COP31 (Antalya) collapsed Sunday 22 Nov: longest COP ever, first
  breakdown since COP15 (2009). The "Antalya Pact" draft never
  mentioned fossil fuels; the EU, UK, Panama, Marshall Islands,
  Norway and least-developed countries rejected it.
- UN chief Guterres "deeply disappointed". Mohamed Adow (Power Shift
  Africa): "worst ever COP". 194 delegations at the talks; the US did
  not take part.
- Sources: CNN, Le Monde, CNBC Africa.

## 4. WORLD COURT — MYANMAR GUILTY OF GENOCIDE (first state ever)
- ICJ, Thursday 19 Nov 2026, 9–6: Myanmar committed genocide against
  the Rohingya in the 2016–2017 "clearance operations" — the first
  time in its 80-year history the court has held a STATE responsible
  for genocide. Ruling final, binding, cannot be appealed.
- ~10,000 Rohingya violently killed. Ordered reparations, safe
  voluntary return, return of property. Case brought by The Gambia
  (2019). Myanmar's military-backed government dismissed the ruling
  as "unfair".
- Sources: HRW, CNN, Le Monde, WSJ, OHCHR.

## 5. SHENZHEN — FIRST XI–TRUMP–PUTIN TRILATERAL
- Thursday 19 Nov, Kylin Villa, Shenzhen (APEC sidelines): first ever
  three-way meeting of the leaders of the three largest nuclear
  powers (~70–90 min). Four-point joint declaration: UN reform,
  refined-petroleum supply, ICC must not "abuse jurisdiction",
  dialogue on reducing nuclear risks ("a nuclear war cannot be won
  and must never be fought").
- No breakthrough on Ukraine or Iran; no timetable to replace expired
  New START. Trump called it "great and highly consequential".
- Sources: RFE/RL, AP, NY Post.

## 6. SPORT — ALCARAZ WINS FIRST ATP FINALS
- Sunday 22 Nov, Turin: Carlos Alcaraz beat world No. 1 Alexander

  tie-break. First ATP Finals title; 14-match win streak; 6th title
  of 2026; 30th career title; ~$5.17M winner's cheque (record).
- First Spanish man to win it since Alex Corretja (1998).
- Sources: theScore, The Times, BBC, ATP Tour.
